# Project Overview

## What this project is

A Power BI dashboard that tracks workforce composition — headcount, income, satisfaction, tenure — and flags which employees or groups are at risk of attrition, using a transparent, rule-based risk score rather than a black-box model.

## Why workforce analytics and attrition analysis matter

Losing an employee is expensive: recruiting, onboarding, and lost productivity during the transition typically cost several months of that role's salary. Organizations that can't see attrition coming are always reacting instead of preventing:

- **Attrition Rate % alone hides the story.** A 15% company-wide rate might mean every department is equally leaky, or it might mean one department is bleeding people while the rest are fine — you only find out by slicing the data.
- **Risk Level is a leading indicator.** Flagging "High" risk employees while they're still on payroll is the whole point of a retention program — waiting for the resignation letter is too late.
- **Overtime, satisfaction, and work-life balance are testable hypotheses, not assumptions.** A dashboard lets HR actually check whether these commonly-blamed factors show up in their own data, instead of guessing.
- **Department and role breakdowns guide where to act.** Retention budget aimed at the wrong team wastes money the same way marketing budget does.

## How companies use dashboards like this one

HR and people analytics teams typically review a dashboard like this monthly or quarterly: checking whether attrition rate moved, which departments need attention, and how many employees just moved into the "High" risk bucket. It becomes the shared reference in workforce planning meetings and retention strategy discussions with leadership.

## Job roles that use this kind of tool

- **HR Analyst** — builds and maintains the workforce reporting layer
- **HR Manager** — uses department-level insights to plan retention initiatives
- **People Analytics Analyst** — owns the attrition risk modeling and trend tracking
- **Business Analyst** — supports HR with broader reporting and data infrastructure
- **Operations Manager** — uses attrition data for headcount and workforce planning

## How MBA and business-analytics students benefit

This project builds fluency in the exact vocabulary HR analytics interviews test: attrition rate, tenure, risk segmentation, department-wise breakdowns — not just a generic "employee dashboard." It also covers the full BI workflow end to end: cleaning messy HR data, modeling it, writing attrition-specific DAX, and building a rule-based (explainable) risk score — a good stepping stone toward more advanced predictive HR analytics later.
