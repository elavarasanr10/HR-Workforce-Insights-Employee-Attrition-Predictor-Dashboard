## Caption — Professional version

Just finished a new Power BI project: HR Workforce Insights & Employee Attrition Predictor Dashboard.

I built this to simulate how HR and people analytics teams actually work — tracking not just how many people left, but which departments, roles, and employee groups are at the highest risk of leaving next.

What went into it:
→ Cleaned and transformed 150+ employee records in Power Query (nulls, duplicates, inconsistent formatting)
→ Built 10+ DAX measures — Attrition Rate %, Department-wise Attrition, Job Role-wise Attrition, High Attrition Risk Employees Count
→ Designed a rule-based Attrition Risk Level (Low/Medium/High) from satisfaction, work-life balance, overtime, and promotion history — fully transparent, no black box
→ Built a dashboard with KPI cards, attrition breakdowns by department and role, and a risk-level view

The most useful output: a clear, explainable list of which currently-employed people are flagged "High" risk — exactly who a retention conversation should happen with first.

## How to post it

1. **Images to upload:** the full dashboard screenshot first, followed by 2–3 close-ups — the KPI card row, the attrition-by-department chart, and the risk-level breakdown.
2. **First line matters most:** LinkedIn truncates after 2–3 lines, so the hook line needs to stand on its own before "see more."
3. **GitHub link placement:** put it in the first comment, not the post body, and mention "link in comments" at the end of the caption — outbound links in the post body get suppressed by the algorithm.
4. **Credibility signals:** name the tools explicitly (Power BI, Power Query, DAX) rather than saying "data tools."
5. **Tag relevant communities/pages sparingly** — don't tag individuals who didn't contribute.
