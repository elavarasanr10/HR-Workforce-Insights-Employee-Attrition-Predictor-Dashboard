# Resume-Ready Project Descriptions

## One-line version

Built a Power BI dashboard tracking employee attrition and flagging at-risk employees across departments and job roles.

## 2–3 line version

Designed and built an HR Workforce Insights & Employee Attrition Predictor dashboard in Power BI, covering 150+ employee records across demographics, compensation, and engagement metrics. Used Power Query for data cleaning and DAX for KPIs including Attrition Rate %, Department-wise Attrition, and a rule-based Attrition Risk Level. Delivered department- and role-level breakdowns to help HR prioritize retention efforts.

## Strong ATS-friendly professional version

Developed an end-to-end HR Workforce Insights & Employee Attrition Predictor dashboard using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to track headcount, compensation, satisfaction, and attrition across departments, job roles, and work modes. Cleaned and transformed raw HR data (handling nulls, duplicates, and inconsistent formatting) into an analysis-ready model, including derived Age Group and Experience Band segments. Built 10+ DAX measures — including Attrition Rate %, Department-wise Attrition, and High Attrition Risk Employees Count — and a multi-visual dashboard (KPI cards, department/role attrition charts, risk breakdown, matrix table) enabling HR to identify at-risk employees and prioritize retention strategy.

## LinkedIn "About this project" blurb (short)

HR Workforce Insights & Employee Attrition Predictor Dashboard — a Power BI project simulating how HR and people analytics teams track attrition and flag at-risk employees using a transparent, rule-based risk score.
